01 — Profile

Inspection, written down

I am a mechanical engineer working in fabrication quality control, with seven years across manufacturing and welding inspection and five of them on rail and steel structures. My work is the unglamorous part that keeps a build safe: fit-up checked before the arc starts, welds inspected during and after, NDT results read and acted on, and every finding written into a report someone can sign.

I hold the International Welding Inspector (Comprehensive) qualification from the IIW / Indonesian ANB and the Ministry of Industry, plus ASNT Level II in ultrasonic, penetrant and magnetic testing through the NDE Center at ITS Surabaya. On site I have led teams of up to 50, run welder qualification and training, kept WPS/WC/BPS and filler databases current, and coordinated day to day with clients and third-party inspectors such as PT Sucofindo, Bureau Veritas, PT Frentech and Applus.

The systems thinking came earlier, in manufacturing: unit head roles at Mayora and Solo Technopark, where GMP, 5R and HACCP discipline — and a habit of measuring before asserting — shaped how I run inspection today.

Welding inspection

Non-destructive testing

ASNT SNT-TC-1A Level II in three methods, applied on production joints and on request, with results written straight into the inspection record.
